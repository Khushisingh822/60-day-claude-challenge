      🚀 60 Days of AI

      Welcome to my 60 Days of AI Learning Journey! 🤖

      Hi, I am Khushi Singh, a B.Tech Computer Science student passionate about technology, coding, and continuous learning.

      I have started this 60-day challenge to explore the world of Artificial Intelligence and improve my technical skills through consistent learning and practical work.

      🎯 My Goals

      • 🤖 Learn and use AI tools effectively
      • 💻 Improve my programming and technical skills
      • 🧠 Understand AI concepts in a simple and practical way
      • 🛠️ Build useful projects using AI
      • 🚀 Improve my problem-solving and productivity
      • 📚 Learn something new every day
      • 📈 Track and document my growth throughout this journey

      📅 My Learning Journey

      This repository will contain my daily learning progress, projects, experiments, notes, screenshots, and experiences throughout the 60 Days of AI Challenge.

      Every day is an opportunity to learn, build, improve, and grow.

      Learn. Build. Experiment. Improve. Repeat. 🔥
