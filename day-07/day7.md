## 📚 What I Learned

* Choosing the right Claude model is more effective than using the same model for every task.
* **Sonnet** is the best balance of speed, quality, and reasoning for daily work.
* **Haiku** is ideal for quick questions and lightweight tasks.
* **Opus** is best for deep research and complex problem-solving.
* Using the appropriate effort level improves both response quality and productivity.

## 🎯 Outcome

Successfully created my personal **Claude Usage Strategy**, a practical AI workflow that helps me choose the right model and effort level for different tasks such as coding, studying, resume building, research, and career preparation.
