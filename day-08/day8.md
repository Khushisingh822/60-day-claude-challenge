# 🌍 Day 8 – Personal Environmental Health Analyzer

## 📅 Day 8 of My 60-Day AI Learning Challenge

Today, I worked on designing a **Personal Environmental Health Analyzer**, an AI-powered dashboard concept that analyzes air quality and water quality data to generate personalized environmental health insights.

## 📌 What I Did Today

* Explored **AQI (Air Quality Index)** and water quality metrics.
* Compared environmental data across multiple cities.
* Designed a modern **interactive dashboard layout**.
* Planned key visualizations including AQI, PM2.5, PM10, and city rankings.
* Created environmental health scorecards and risk indicators.
* Added personalized recommendations for air quality, water quality, hair health, and skin health.

## 🛠️ Tools Used

* Claude AI
* ChatGPT
* Canva
* GitHub

## 📚 What I Learned

* AQI values directly impact respiratory health, energy levels, and exercise performance.
* PM2.5 and PM10 are important indicators of air pollution.
* Water quality can influence **hair fall, scalp health, skin dryness, and acne**.
* Visual dashboards make environmental data easier to understand and compare.
* Combining analytics with personalized health recommendations creates a more meaningful user experience.

## 🎯 Outcome

Successfully designed a **Personal Environmental Health Analyzer dashboard concept** featuring environmental metrics, interactive insights, health risk indicators, and personalized recommendations for cleaner and healthier living.
