# Day 14: AI Job Red Flag Detector

## Overview

Today I explored how AI can act as a **Job Red Flag Detector** that helps identify risky job opportunities before applying. Instead of evaluating whether I was a fit for a company, I asked AI to evaluate whether a company and job description were a good fit for me.

## What I Asked AI To Do

I asked AI to analyze a **Job Description and Company Information** and identify:

* Unrealistic requirements
* Toxic workplace signals
* Remote job authenticity
* Hiring risks
* Company stability concerns

## Key Learnings

* A job posting can reveal a lot about a company’s work culture.
* Requirements such as **3–5 years of experience for an entry-level role**, excessive responsibilities, or contradictory expectations are important warning signs.
* Phrases like **“wear many hats,” “fast-paced environment,” “rockstar developer,”** and **“hustle culture”** may indicate potential burnout or poor work-life balance.
* Missing salary information, vague responsibilities, and unclear remote policies should be treated carefully.

## Why This Was Valuable

As a **B.Tech Computer Science student preparing for software engineering roles**, I realized that job searching is not only about getting selected—it is also about avoiding opportunities that may not support long-term career growth.

## Outcome

AI generated a structured **Job Risk Analysis Report** including:

* Overall Risk Score
* Top Red Flags
* Positive Signals
* Risk Breakdown
* Final Verdict (Apply / Apply with Caution / Avoid)
* Smart Interview Questions to validate concerns

## Tools Used

* ChatGPT (AI Job Red Flag Detector)
* LinkedIn
* GitHub
* Job Descriptions from software engineering roles

## Reflection

Today’s challenge taught me that **a good developer should also become a smart job seeker**. AI can help evaluate opportunities objectively and avoid wasting time on misleading or unhealthy job environments.
