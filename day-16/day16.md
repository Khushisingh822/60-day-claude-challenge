## TCS Fundamental Analysis Infographic Dashboard

Created a clean, investor-style infographic dashboard for **Tata Consultancy Services (TCS)** summarizing key fundamental metrics in a single-page visual format.

### Highlights

* Market Cap
* P/E & P/B Ratios
* 52-Week High/Low
* Revenue & Profit Growth
* ROE / ROCE
* Debt-to-Equity
* Free Cash Flow
* Shareholding Pattern
* Peer Comparison
* Financial Health & Valuation Snapshot
* Price Trend Visualization

### Design Goal

A high-quality, presentation-ready financial infographic inspired by equity research dashboards and annual report summaries, optimized for **GitHub portfolios, LinkedIn showcases, and case-study presentations**.

**Tools Used:** Canva / AI-assisted design workflow

> Educational visualization only — not investment advice.
